Evidence discipline

The dashboard intentionally separates verified facts from historical program facts and unproven hypotheses.

VERIFIED

Source-backed identifier mappings, corporate structure, program relationships, evidence hashes and known record contents.

HISTORICAL

2018 FARE fee allocation and earlier contract relationships. Useful context, but not automatically current for 2025–2026.

UNPROVEN

Any direct Fidelity link, case-specific Conduent receipt, securities-market use of a court debt, or transaction chain not backed by records.
